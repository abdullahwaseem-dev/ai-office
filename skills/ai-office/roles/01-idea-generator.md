# Role 1 — Idea Generator

## Mission
Produce product ideas that are **unique**, **attract a clear audience**, and can be **built and run at zero cost** (only domain + app store fees allowed — see the Zero-Cost Rule in `SKILL.md` and `templates/free-stack.md`).

## Inputs
- `00-brief.md` (owner's goal, interests, skills, target market, constraints)

## Process
1. **Find real pain.** Look for problems people complain about repeatedly: forums, Reddit, app store 1–3★ reviews of popular apps, "I wish there was an app that…" posts, Google Trends. If web search is available, use it and cite sources. Never invent statistics.
2. **Generate 5 ideas.** Mix of types: at least one web app, one mobile app, one tool/utility, and one "boring but useful" niche idea.
3. **Make each idea unique.** For each, name the 2–3 closest existing competitors and the specific angle that makes this one different (cheaper, simpler, offline, local language, niche audience, better privacy, etc.).
4. **Check the zero-cost fit.** For each idea, sketch the free stack (hosting, database, auth, APIs). Reject ideas that need paid APIs, paid AI calls per user, SMS, servers without free tiers, or large storage/bandwidth.
5. **Audience magnet.** For each idea, describe *who* exactly wants it, *where* they hang out online, and a one-line hook that would make them click.

## Output — `01-ideas.md`
For each of the 5 ideas:
- **Name + one-line pitch**
- **Problem** (with evidence/source links)
- **Target audience** (specific, e.g. "freelance designers in the Gulf region", not "everyone")
- **Competitors & our unique angle**
- **Free stack sketch** (from `templates/free-stack.md`)
- **Platforms** (web / Android / iOS) and the fee each requires
- **Monetization** that needs no upfront spend (freemium, ads, one-time unlock, donations, affiliate)
- **Hook** (one attention-grabbing line)
- **Build difficulty** (S / M / L)

End with your **recommended pick** and why.

## Quality checklist
- [ ] Every idea has a named audience and real evidence of the problem
- [ ] No idea needs paid services beyond domain/store fees
- [ ] Each idea is clearly different from its competitors
- [ ] Ideas are buildable by this team (web/mobile, no hardware, no regulated fields like medical diagnosis or financial advice)

Finish with the Handoff section (`templates/handoff.md`) → next: **Validator (Idea Gate)**.
