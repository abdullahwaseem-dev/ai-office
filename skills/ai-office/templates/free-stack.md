# Approved Free Stack

Free tiers change. Before relying on a limit, check the provider's current pricing page and note the date checked. Prefer services that **stop** at the free limit instead of billing automatically.

## Allowed paid items (the only ones)
| Item | Typical cost |
|---|---|
| Domain name | ~$10–15/year (Cloudflare Registrar, Porkbun, Namecheap) |
| Google Play Console | $25 one-time |
| Apple Developer Program | $99/year |

## Hosting (frontend / static sites)
Cloudflare Pages · GitHub Pages · Vercel (Hobby) · Netlify (Free)

## Backend, database, auth
Supabase (Free) · Firebase (Spark plan — no billing account attached) · Cloudflare Workers + D1 + KV · PocketBase on a free host · On-device storage (SQLite / localStorage) when no server is needed

## Mobile
Expo (React Native) · Flutter · PWA (no store fee at all)

## Email & newsletters
Free tiers of Resend / Brevo / Buttondown / MailerLite (check sending limits)

## Analytics
Google Search Console · Google Play Console / App Store Connect analytics · Cloudflare Web Analytics · free tiers of privacy-friendly tools

## Payments (pay-per-transaction only, no monthly fee)
App store in-app purchases · Stripe · Lemon Squeezy · Paddle · Gumroad

## Design, video & media
Google Fonts · Figma (free) / Penpot · Inkscape · GIMP · Canva free · DaVinci Resolve (free) · CapCut · ffmpeg · OBS · Pixabay / Unsplash / Pexels (check license) · YouTube Audio Library

## Dev & testing
GitHub (repos, Actions free minutes) · Playwright · Vitest/Jest · Lighthouse · axe accessibility checker

## Not allowed without owner approval
Pay-as-you-go AI/LLM APIs inside the product · SMS/phone verification · paid ads · paid plugins or themes · any service requiring a credit card with no spending cap
