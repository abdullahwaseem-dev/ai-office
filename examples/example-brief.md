```
/ai-office I want a zero-cost mobile app for university students in Saudi Arabia
that helps them split shared expenses with roommates. Arabic + English.
I can post on Instagram and LinkedIn myself but I can't film videos of my face.
Build the MVP and prepare the full launch kit.
```

## What the Project Manager will turn it into (`00-brief.md`, shortened)

- **Goal:** MVP of a roommate expense-splitting app + launch kit
- **Audience:** university students sharing apartments, KSA; Arabic (RTL) + English
- **Platforms:** Android + iOS (Expo), plus a landing page on Cloudflare Pages
- **Owner's skills:** posts on Instagram and LinkedIn; no on-camera video, so the Video Editor uses screen recordings + animated text
- **Budget:** domain + Play ($25) + Apple ($99/yr); everything else free (Supabase free tier, no SMS verification)
- **Roles:** all 13 active
- **Success:** MVP in stores, 200 signups in 30 days

Then Phase 1 starts: the Idea Generator refines the concept and checks competitors, and the Validator scores it.
